CRITERIA: photorealism, reference_style.
REFERENCE is a still image supplying photographic appearance, NOT motion/geometry.
CANDIDATE is the generated video. For every evidence entry ADD reference_observation:
describe an observable photographic property in the image and its agreement or
disagreement with the candidate. Object/pose/composition copying earns no style credit.
A SOURCE RENDERING image is also supplied as a contrast aid, not as another candidate.
Use it to distinguish retained proxy-rendering cues from a convincing transformation
toward the reference's photographic material/lighting treatment. Score the candidate
only. Source is NOT automatically zero and reference is NOT automatically100:
both still need visible photographic evidence. Do not transfer source defects to
the candidate. Compare the actual materials and light, not scene identity.

photorealism: inspect natural surface microvariation vs flat/painted/plastic surfaces;
plausible light transport, shadow softness/contact, reflections and highlights;
organic detail and geometry vs conspicuous low-poly/blocky silhouettes; photographic
depth, exposure and material response vs game-engine/cel-shaded illustration.
One attractive first frame does not excuse a later shift to synthetic-looking footage.
Do not label all 3D content fake merely because it is computer-generated; evaluate
visible photographic realism. Do not equate fantasy, saturated colors, low FPS,
low resolution or compression by themselves with non-photorealistic rendering.
reference_style: compare material/light/color treatment and photographic aesthetic,
not exact placement, identity or narrative contents. First state whether the image
itself is photographic, mixed, or clearly stylized, citing visible evidence.
Candidate stills and the reference are supplied at the same maximum viewing size.
Judge rendering characteristics at that common scale, not missing fine detail
inferred from resolution/FPS metadata. The opening candidate may closely match
the reference: inspect before claiming contradictory photographic properties.
Do not call grass blocky unless actual geometric facets/silhouettes are visible;
blur, grass-blade shape or sparse texture alone is not proof of low-poly geometry.
Do not assume a reference is photographic merely because it is labeled REFERENCE.
These are still views: do not invent collision response, acceleration or movement
from them. Photographic material/light evidence is needed for Style deductions.
Motion discontinuities or object persistence failures alone belong to Quality.
Do not describe the candidate as fake just because it was generated. A coherent
photographic treatment with small synthesized details belongs in the high band,
not the middle band, unless concrete proxy-like cues dominate the viewing experience.
If the reference is stylized, matching it may raise reference_style but must NOT
raise photorealism. The final Style score NEVER exceeds the photorealism subscore.
The numeric rule is min(photorealism, 0.7*photorealism + 0.3*reference_style).
Synthetic appearance belongs here; temporal melting/flicker belongs primarily
to Quality. State overlap only if both distinct failures are visibly supported.
